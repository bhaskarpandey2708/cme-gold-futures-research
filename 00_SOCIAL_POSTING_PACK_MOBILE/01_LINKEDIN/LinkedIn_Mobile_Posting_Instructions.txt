HOW TO POST THE 7-SLIDE PDF CAROUSEL ON LINKEDIN MOBILE APP
=============================================================================

1. Open the LinkedIn App on your iPhone or Android.
2. Tap the '+' button at the bottom to create a new post.
3. Tap the '...' (More) icon at the bottom of the composer.
4. Select 'Add a document' (or 'Document' icon).
5. Browse your files and select:
   04_CAROUSEL_PDF_AND_SLIDES/Apex_CME_Globex_Institutional_Walkthrough.pdf
6. In the Document Title field, enter:
   "Apex CME Globex Institutional Architecture Walkthrough"
7. In the post text area, paste the entire text from:
   01_LINKEDIN/Post_1_Launch_Document_Carousel.txt
8. Review and tap 'Post'!

Why Document Posts Win on LinkedIn:
• PDF Document posts generate 3x to 5x higher reach than standard link posts.
• Allocators and quants love swiping through slide decks directly inside their mobile feed.
• Every slide has high-resolution charts, system architectures, and contact links.
